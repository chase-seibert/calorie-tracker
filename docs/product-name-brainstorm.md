# Product name brainstorm

The naming idea is a tracker for people who are highly intentional about a few
specific fitness or nutrition signals, rather than a sprawling health dashboard.
The name should feel focused, fast, and confident without promising medical
outcomes.

## Recommended direction

**Dialed**

“Dialed” is short, familiar in fitness culture, and maps cleanly to the product:
users dial in the metrics that matter to them. It can stretch beyond calories as
the app adds optional focus areas without sounding like a generic food database.

Possible positioning lines:

- Dial in what matters.
- Nutrition tracking, dialed to you.
- Track less. Focus more.

## Shortlist

1. **Dialed** — strongest combination of fitness energy and configurable focus.
2. **MacroLens** — clearly communicates selective nutrition visibility, but is
   more descriptive and less ownable in tone.
3. **Setpoint** — suggests targets and discipline; slightly more clinical.
4. **FocusFuel** — clear fitness and nutrition connection; more energetic but
   less premium.
5. **Intent** — broad and calm, though it needs a nutrition-oriented subtitle.
6. **Signal** — emphasizes separating meaningful trends from noise; broad enough
   to require explanatory branding.
7. **Cutline** — performance-oriented and memorable, but leans toward weight loss
   and cutting phases.
8. **Narrow** — distinctive expression of deliberate focus, though the word can
   sound restrictive.

## Naming guardrails

- Avoid names centered only on calories if selectable focus areas are a core
  differentiator.
- Avoid medical or diagnostic language.
- Prefer one or two words that remain readable under an iPhone app icon.
- Check App Store, domain, and trademark availability before choosing a final
  name; this brainstorm makes no availability claims.
