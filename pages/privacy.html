Yum Chase is designed so the everyday food log stays on your device. Some optional features use connected services; this page explains what leaves your device and when.

01

On your device

Your diary entries, personal foods, saved meals, and remembered quantities are stored in Yum Chase’s local app database. The food catalog and search index are bundled with the app, so routine food logging and catalog search do not need an app server or an internet connection.

Yum Chase does not read information from Apple Health. If you choose to use the optional services described below, those features send the information needed for that action.

02

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Yum Chase can write nutrition information such as calories, carbohydrates, fat, protein, and fiber. The app requests permission to share those nutrition types and does not request permission to read Health data. Apple Health data is managed by Apple and your device settings.

03

AI meal analysis

When you choose Analyze or Retry, Yum Chase sends the meal description you entered and, if selected, a prepared JPEG photo to the inference service configured for the app. The request does not include your diary history or HealthKit history. Nothing is logged until you review the estimate and choose Log.

The configured service may use an AI model provider to produce the estimate. Provider processing and retention depend on the service and provider terms; this draft does not claim zero retention. Confirm the production service, provider, retention terms, and contact route before release.

04

Weekly reports

If you generate a weekly report with the configured OpenAI route, the request includes up to 21 completed local days of calories, protein, known fiber, logging counts, marked cheat or gap days, and estimated cheat-day overages; up to 12 individual food highlights; your report prompt; and enabled app guidance. HealthKit data and diary entry notes are excluded.

When you choose Apple’s on-device Foundation Model and it is available, the report runs on the device. Report generation is optional.

05

Analytics and feedback

When analytics is enabled in the installed build, Yum Chase sends feature-use events, catalog or local item identifiers, successful and failed food search terms, searches dismissed without logging, and unmatched barcodes to its analytics service. If you submit food feedback, the text you enter is included with that feedback event.

Analytics events do not include diary food names, meal contents, quantities, calorie totals, or Health data. Analytics availability and configuration can vary by build.

06

Apple Reminders

If you connect Apple Reminders, only snack suggestions you select and their serving tips are added when you tap Add. The selected list may sync through your Apple account or be shared with people you choose. Your diary and focus settings are not exported with those reminders.

07

This website

This static website has no sign-in, contact form, or embedded analytics. The hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver the pages; confirm the provider’s logging and retention details before public release.
